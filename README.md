# Tiny Bake House (Unity)

A 3D isometric baking game made by a team of 5 for our semester project at Seamedu.
Run a bakery by day, then hunt ingredient creatures in 2D side-scrolling worlds
to collect what you need for your recipes.

> 🔧 **Active development is on the [`beta` branch](https://github.com/3Azure-dev/IsometricBakeHouse/tree/beta).**
> `main` is updated when a milestone is stable.

## Gameplay
- **Bakery (3D isometric):** serve customers, take orders, bake in ovens and stoves,
  deliver food, buy equipment and furniture in the shop, day/night clock
- **Ingredient worlds (2D):** Bread, Cupcake and Pizza worlds — hunt creatures like
  Butter, Egg, Sauce, Sugar, Cheese, Dough and Seeds for their ingredient drops
- Wand-based combat, moving platforms, hazards like molten cheese pools and slow puddles
